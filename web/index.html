Straight answers

Questions people ask first.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, no trial clock and nothing to unlock. The app is free to use.

What does signing in involve?

Your phone number and an OTP, or your Google account — whichever you prefer. That is the whole of it: no password to invent, no email to confirm. Signing in is what keeps the household on every family member's phone and safe if you lose yours.

What happens to my data if I change my phone?

Nothing to do. Your household lives in the cloud, so you sign in on the new phone and everything is already there — every person, every advance, every loan balance.

Can I overrule what the app works out?

Always. Take part of a loan instalment instead of the whole one and the rest waits for next month. Skip a deduction entirely for someone having a hard month. Type over any rate it suggests. Correct a day you marked wrong three weeks ago. Undo a salary you have already recorded. The app does the arithmetic — you decide the number.

Will it work when the internet is down?

Yes. You can add people, mark attendance and record advances completely offline. Anything you do is sent up automatically the next time you have a connection.

Is my staff's Aadhaar and bank information safe?

They sit in a separate section on the person's own profile, and only the family members you have invited into your household can read them. Nobody else — not another household, not a stranger with the code to somewhere else.

My mother is not comfortable with apps. Can she use it?

That is who it was designed around. There is a guided walkthrough the first time you open it that points at the exact button to press, a large-text mode, and the whole thing works in Hindi.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It opens in your browser. On Android or iPhone you can also use “Add to Home Screen” and it will behave like any other app.
